Our Services

Artifact-as-Code

Artifact-as-Code is a transformative approach that integrates software artifacts into the development process as code. This enables better traceability, versioning, and management of artifacts throughout the lifecycle of a project. By treating artifacts as code, teams can automate their deployments and ensure consistency, reducing manual errors and enhancing quality.

AI Act Gap Analysis

The AI Act Gap Analysis service provides organizations with a comprehensive evaluation of their AI systems against regulatory requirements set forth by the European Union’s AI Act. This analysis identifies critical gaps in compliance, ensuring that businesses can adapt their AI systems to meet legal standards while promoting ethical AI use.

ASPICE-to-AI-Act Mapping

Our ASPICE-to-AI-Act Mapping service offers a structured pathway for organizations seeking to align their automotive software development processes (ASPICE) with the requirements of the AI Act. This mapping helps businesses not only comply with regulations but also enhances their software development practices, ensuring high standards for both safety and performance in AI-driven systems.
